🔮 Tarot Readings
Draw a 1, 3, or 5-card spread with a real face-down flip animation. Every card includes upright and reversed meanings, a spread narrative, and reflection prompts to help the reading actually mean something to you.

🔢 Numerology
Get your Life Path, Soul Urge, and Personality numbers from your name and birthdate, plus your Personal Year and Personal Day — the same calculation chain, carried forward to today.

Ten planet-based reflection themes tied to your own chart, with a recurring-pattern banner that surfaces themes you keep coming back to.

💤 Dream Interpreter
A built-in symbol dictionary (28+ symbols) to help you unpack recurring dream imagery.

📅 Today
A daily card of the day plus a real convergence check — it only flags a connection when your card's number actually echoes your Personal Day number, or its element matches today's Moon. No forced coincidences.

- No ads. No analytics trackers. No selling your data.
