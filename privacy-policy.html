SoulSite Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

SoulSite ("the app", "we") is a personal numerology, tarot, and astrology reflection app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

Creating an account and using certain features may involve the following information, all of it entered directly by you:

  • Account info: your email address and display name, and a profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Birth details: birthdate, and optionally birth time and birth city/coordinates, used to calculate your numerology and astrology results (Sun/Moon/Rising sign, Life Path number, Personal Year, etc.).
  • Saved content: tarot readings, shadow-work journal entries, and favorited angel numbers that you choose to save to your account.

None of this is required to use the app — every calculator and reading works without an account. Signing in only adds the ability to save your results and have them follow you across sessions and devices.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to run the features you're using: calculating your numerology/astrology results and letting you retrieve your own saved readings, journal entries, and favorites later. We do not use your data for advertising, and the app does not contain any advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.

Where it's stored

Account and saved-content data is stored using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud infrastructure). Each user's saved data is private to their own account — access is restricted at the database level so only you can read or write your own information.

Third parties

The only third-party service involved is Google/Firebase, used for sign-in and data storage as described above. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or other third parties.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the Profile menu. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we'll remove it.

Children's privacy

SoulSite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
